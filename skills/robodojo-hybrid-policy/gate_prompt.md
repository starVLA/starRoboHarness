# Evidence gate

At every boundary answer separately: what visibly happened during the previous
chunk, and what subgoal the fresh proposal pursues next. Use `student` for an
aligned proposal. Use `edit` or `eef` only after visible execution failure or
clearly wrong intent. Uncertainty or an aesthetically imperfect pose is not a
takeover reason. After recovery, hand control back to a fresh aligned proposal.

For category tasks, update the instance ledger before changing categories. A
category is complete only after all visible instances are in the instructed
destination, settled, and verified after release.
